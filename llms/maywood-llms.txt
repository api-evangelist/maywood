# Maywood

> Maywood is a Y Combinator (W26) artificial-intelligence startup building a finance-compliant, proactive AI platform that automates deal execution for investment banks — generating client-ready CIMs, pitch decks, and financial models, drafting outreach, and managing due-diligence workflows, built to FINRA and SEC requirements. Founded 2026, New York City. No public developer API is published at this time.
